Questions

Before you book.

What is the taxi fare from Chandigarh to Delhi Airport?

A sedan (Dzire or Etios) is ₹3,500 plus parking, an Ertiga is ₹4,500 and an Innova is ₹5,500.

What is the taxi fare from Chandigarh to Manali?

A sedan is ₹4,000 plus parking, an Ertiga is ₹5,000 and an Innova is ₹6,000.

What is the taxi fare from Chandigarh to Shimla, Amritsar or Pathankot?

For each of these routes a sedan is ₹2,500 plus parking, an Ertiga is ₹3,500 and an Innova is ₹4,500.

Do you have a Tempo Traveller?

Yes, for every route on this page. The price depends on the date and group size, so we quote it on request.

How do I book?

Call us or send a WhatsApp message with your route, date and number of passengers. We confirm the car and fare on the same chat.
